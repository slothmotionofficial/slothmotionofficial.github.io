Privacy Policy

YouTube Daily Intelligence · Effective 9 November 2026

YouTube Daily Intelligence ("the app") is a personal tool used only by the owner of the Sloth Motion YouTube channel to analyse that channel. This policy explains what data the app accesses and how it is handled.

Data the app accesses

With the signed-in user's permission, the app reads, through Google's official APIs:

  • Basic channel and video information (titles, publish dates, durations, public counts) via the YouTube Data API.
  • Channel and video analytics (views, watch time, subscribers, retention, traffic sources, countries, and estimated revenue where available) via the YouTube Analytics API.

Access is read-only. The app cannot upload, modify or delete any YouTube content or settings.

How data is used

The data is used only to generate a private daily performance report for the channel owner. It is not used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

Storage

  • All data and reports are stored only on the owner's own computer.
  • The Google sign-in token is stored encrypted on that computer and is never shared.
  • The app has no server and does not upload data to any database.

Sharing

The app does not sell, rent or share Google user data with anyone. The owner may use an AI assistant (Claude by Anthropic), running under the owner's own account, to write the analysis section of their own report.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app also uses YouTube API Services, subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access and deleting data

  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Local data is deleted by removing the app's folder from the owner's computer.

Contact
