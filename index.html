YouTube Daily Intelligence

A personal tool that turns the owner's own YouTube channel analytics into a short daily briefing.

Who it's for: this app is used only by the owner of the Sloth Motion YouTube channel to analyse their own channel. It is not offered to the public.

What it does

  • Reads the owner's channel statistics through Google's official YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API.
  • Produces a daily report: views, watch time, subscribers, retention, traffic sources and (if available) estimated revenue.
  • Runs entirely on the owner's own Windows PC. There is no server and no website account.

What it does not do

  • It has read-only access. It cannot upload, edit, delete, comment or change anything on YouTube.
  • It does not sell, share or publish anyone's data.

Google permissions requested

  • youtube.readonly: view your YouTube account
  • yt-analytics-monetary.readonly: view YouTube Analytics reports, including revenue
